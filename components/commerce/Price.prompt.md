Supermarket-style price block with raised dollar sign and cents, plus optional was-price and savings line.

```jsx
<Price value={12.98} />
<Price value={12.98} was={16.98} size="lg" />
```

`value` (required), `was` (strikethrough + auto savings), `savings` (override), `size` `sm|md|lg`, `align` `left|center`.

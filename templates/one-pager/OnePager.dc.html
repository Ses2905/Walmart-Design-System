The problem

Describe the customer problem in concrete terms — who hits it, how often, and what it costs them today. Name the evidence: support volume, funnel drop-off, research quotes. Two or three sentences is enough; the detail belongs in the appendix, not here.

The approach

State the bet in one sentence, then the shape of the solution. Be explicit about what you are not doing.

  • First capability, described by what the customer can now do
  • Second capability, same framing
  • Explicitly out of scope for this release

Why now

The timing argument — a platform change, a competitive move, a cost curve, a policy shift. If there is no forcing function, say so honestly.
